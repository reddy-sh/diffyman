# 026-blank-line-changes

Only blank lines change: the right side adds a second blank line after the heading, drops one of the two blank lines before the last paragraph, and appends a blank line at the end. `ignore.blankLines` is `false`, so every blank-line change is a real change at line granularity (case 036 runs the same inputs with `true`). A correct result with `context: 1` has two hunks. h1: equal L2/R2, an `insert` of R3 (`leftRange {start: 3, count: 0}`), equal L3/R4. h2: equal L7/R8, a `delete` of L8, equal L9/R9, and an `insert` appending R10 (`leftRange {start: 10, count: 0}`). Stats show `linesAdded: 2`, `linesRemoved: 1`, `linesUnchanged: 8` and `similarity: 0.8421`. Which blank line of an equal pair is reported is the histogram choice; no slider heuristic is applied (spec 7.3).
